🛡 CyberShield

CyberShield

A phishing-detection and digital-security awareness platform that helps everyday users recognise threats, secure their accounts, and report incidents.

Tip of the moment:

Project Objective

Primary goal

To reduce the number of people who fall victim to phishing and weak-authentication attacks by giving them a simple web tool that analyses suspicious messages, advises on stronger credentials, and lets them report threats.

Specific aims

  • Raise awareness of common cyber threats and how to spot them.
  • Give instant, understandable risk feedback on suspicious links and messages.
  • Encourage secure authentication habits, including multi-factor authentication.
  • Offer a simple channel for reporting incidents.

Global relevance

Cyber attacks cross borders and affect individuals, organisations and infrastructure worldwide. The project supports SDG 4 (Quality Education) and SDG 8 (Decent Work and Economic Growth).

System Block Diagram

Explanation

The user works in a web browser. The presentation layer shows the menu and pages. The application logic layer processes links, passwords and reports. The data and services layer holds the indicator rules, submitted reports and awareness content, plus a future external threat-intelligence service. Results return to the user as scores, messages and recommendations.
